US foreign earned income exclusion: the 330 full days test

The physical presence test for the foreign earned income exclusion requires you to spend at least 330 full days in foreign countries (one or several) during a single stretch of 12 consecutive months. Those days can be spread out and need not be consecutive. A full day is 24 consecutive hours, from midnight to midnight, in a foreign country. You also need a foreign tax home and foreign earned income.

Quick answers

What counts as a full day?

A full day is 24 consecutive hours from midnight to midnight spent in a foreign country. Time over international waters does not count as time in a foreign country.

Which 12 months can I use?

Any period of 12 consecutive months, starting on any day and ending on the day preceding the same calendar date one year on. It can overlap other periods and you can pick the one that gives the largest exclusion.

Do days lost to travel matter?

Travel between foreign locations does not cost full days, but travel outside foreign countries that takes 24 hours or more does. A pass through the US lasting under 24 hours, between two points that are both outside the US, does not count as presence in the US.

Is there a waiver?

The 330-day minimum can be waived if war, civil unrest or comparable adverse conditions forced you to leave a foreign country. The IRS publishes the eligible countries and dates each year.

Is there another way to qualify?

Yes, the bona fide residence test. This page covers only the physical presence test.

Worked examples from the IRS

First full day. You leave the US for France on 10 June and arrive at 9:00 a.m. on 11 June. Your first full day in France is 12 June.

Choosing the period. You live and work in New Zealand from 1 January 2024 to 31 August 2025, with 28 days of US vacation in February of each year. For 2024, the qualifying period is 1 January to 31 December 2024. For 2025 you may choose 1 September 2024 to 31 August 2025.

The same arithmetic applies to any window: count the full days you were abroad and check that the total reaches 330.
