UK Statutory Residence Test: how days are counted

For the UK Statutory Residence Test (SRT), a day counts as a day spent in the UK if you are present in the UK as that day ends (midnight). There are exceptions: the deeming rule can add days you were not here at midnight, arrival days spent in transit are not counted, and days caused by exceptional circumstances beyond your control can be ignored for part of the test.

Quick answers

Which period is counted?

The UK tax year, which runs from 6 April to 5 April.

If I land in the morning and fly out the same evening, is that a UK day?

Not under the midnight rule, because you are no longer in the UK when the day ends. The deeming rule can still make some such days count if it applies to you.

What is the deeming rule?

HMRC says it applies if you were resident in the UK for at least one of the three prior tax years, you have at least three UK ties for the year, and you were in the UK on more than 30 days without being here at the end of the day. Each such day after the first 30 is then treated as a day spent in the UK.

Does spending 183 days make me UK resident?

Yes. Spending 183 or more days in the UK in the tax year is the first automatic UK test, so no other test needs considering.

What are the low-day thresholds?

You are automatically non-resident if you are here for fewer than 16 days and were resident in the UK in at least one of the three prior tax years, or here for fewer than 46 days and resident in none of them. The automatic overseas test for full-time work overseas (the third one) has its own 91-day limit. The sufficient ties test applies if no automatic test decides it.

Worked example

You arrive on Monday at 14:00, stay through Tuesday and leave on Wednesday at 09:00.

  • Monday: in the UK at midnight, so it counts (1).
  • Tuesday: in the UK at midnight, so it counts (2).
  • Wednesday: you left before midnight, so it does not count.

Total: 2 days spent in the UK, before any deeming-rule or exceptional-circumstances adjustment. A day trip landing at 09:00 and leaving at 20:00 adds 0.
