Schengen 90/180 rule: how the days are counted

Over any 180-day period you can spend at most 90 days in the Schengen area. For each day of your stay, count back 180 days; the days you spent in the Schengen area in that window must not total more than 90. Your entry day and your exit day both count as days of stay.

Quick answers

Is the 180-day period a fixed half-year?

No. It is a rolling window. The European Commission says to count back 180 days from each day of your stay and make sure the total does not exceed 90.

Do my arrival day and my departure day count?

Yes. Per Article 6 of the Schengen Borders Code (EU Regulation 2016/399), the date of entry is the first day of stay and the date of exit is the last day of stay.

When do days come back?

A day of stay counts for the 180 days starting with that day, so it stops counting on the 180th day after it. A stay day on 1 January 2026 is out of the window from 30 June 2026.

Which stays are not counted?

Time spent on an EU residence permit or a long-stay (D-type) visa is not subject to the 90/180 rule and is not entered in the Commission's calculator. If you hold a short-stay visa, also check the period of authorised stay printed on the visa sticker, because it may be shorter than 90 days.

Which countries are covered?

The Schengen area has 29 countries: 25 EU Member States plus Iceland, Norway, Switzerland and Liechtenstein, according to the European Commission. Check its page for the current list.

Worked example

You are in the Schengen area from 1 January to 31 March 2026. That is 31 + 28 + 31 = 90 days, counting both the entry day and the exit day.

  • On 31 March 2026 you have used 90 days and have 0 left.
  • 1 January 2026 leaves the window on 30 June 2026, so on that date you get 1 day back, then one more each following day.
  • 31 March 2026 leaves the window on 27 September 2026, when all 90 days are back.

A second case: 1 January to 1 March 2026 is 31 + 28 + 1 = 60 days. On 1 March you have 30 days left, so staying on, your 90th and last permitted day is 31 March 2026.
