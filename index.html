Residency Tracker

Keeps a running tally of your days in each country, so you can check and stay under tax-residency and visa day limits: 183-day rules, Schengen 90/180, the US Substantial Presence Test and the UK's Statutory Residence Test.

Private by design

No account and no analytics. Your locations are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Honest about gaps

Days the app can't be sure about are shown as unknown for you to confirm, never guessed.

Free Schengen calculator

Check your 90/180 days in the browser: days used, days left, last safe day and when days come back. Open the calculator or read the explainers.

Day counts only, not tax or legal advice.
