Think a count is wrong? Open the rule in the app and tap Flag this count, or open the day and tap Flag a wrong day. It prefills an email with the rule, period and counts (or the day and its countries) and the app version. Add where you actually were if you want; no coordinates or location history are attached. Without a mail app, the report is copied so you can paste it into any email.

Days aren't being counted in the background

In iOS Settings → Residency Tracker → Location, choose Always. iOS may ask you to confirm Always later; until then the app shows "Waiting for iOS to confirm Always". Low Power Mode can delay updates.

A day shows as unknown

The app never guesses when your phone was off or you moved between countries without a location update. Open the day and tell it where you were, or rebuild past days from your photos in Settings.

Restoring Schengen or Pro

Schengen and Pro are one-time purchases. On a new iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. It brings back whichever you bought, including an upgrade from Schengen to Pro. Family Sharing members get them automatically.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your history moves with an iPhone backup or transfer. You can also tap Back up history in Settings → Backup, then Restore from backup on the new phone.
