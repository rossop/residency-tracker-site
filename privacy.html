Privacy policy

Residency Tracker for iPhone. Last updated 20 October 2026.

In short

We don't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app records stays on your iPhone.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location to work out which country you are in each day. It uses iOS visit and significant-location-change monitoring rather than continuous GPS. Each location is matched to a country using maps built into the app, on your iPhone. Locations are stored on your iPhone and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

If you choose to rebuild past travel from your photos, the app reads the date and place saved in each photo, on your iPhone. Photos are never copied, uploaded or changed.

Files you import or export

You can import travel history from files you pick (for example a calendar or a Google Maps Timeline export) and export reports or a backup. Exports go only where you send them, for example to Mail, Files or another app you choose from the share sheet. We never receive them.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment or personal details from it.

Contacting us

If you email us, for example with "Flag this count" in the app, we receive only what is in your email. The app prefills the rule and its counts, or the day and the countries recorded for it, plus the app and iOS version; it never attaches coordinates or your location history. We use your email only to reply and delete it when the conversation is over.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your iPhone, deleting the app deletes it. Backups you exported are files you control: delete them wherever you saved them. Your data is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you use one, under Apple's terms.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: nomad.daydream177@passmail.net.
