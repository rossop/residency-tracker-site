How it counts

  • Both your entry day and your exit day count as days of stay.
  • The count covers the 180 days up to and including the check date.
  • Overlapping or back-to-back trips are never counted twice.

Read the full rule in the Schengen 90/180 explainer. Day counts only, not immigration or legal advice. It does not account for visas with a shorter stay, residence permits, long-stay visas or bilateral agreements, and border officers decide: check with an adviser or the official EU short-stay calculator.
